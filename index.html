How it works

From quote to driveway in three stops

  1. Stop 1 · Quote

    Tell us the route and the vehicle

    Enter pickup and delivery ZIP codes, your vehicle and when it's ready. Your price comes back right away.

  2. Stop 2 · Pickup

    We assign a carrier and set the window

    Book online, choose open or enclosed, and we confirm the pickup window. The driver inspects the vehicle with you and notes its condition on the bill of lading.

    Your vehicle arrives at your door

    The driver calls ahead, unloads at your address or as close as a truck can legally get, and you inspect and sign for the vehicle.

Services

Open or enclosed: which one do you need?

Most vehicles ship on an open trailer. Enclosed is worth the extra cost when the vehicle itself is the thing you're protecting.

FeatureOpen transportEnclosed transport
Best forDaily drivers, family vehicles, dealer and auction purchasesLuxury, exotic, classic and low-clearance vehicles
ProtectionExposed to weather and road dust, the same as driving itFully covered from weather and road debris
CostThe lower-cost optionCosts more than open on the same route
AvailabilityMost trucks on the road, so pickup is usually fasterFewer trucks, so allow more lead time

Pricing

What goes into your price

No two shipments price the same. The quote tool weighs all of these for your exact move.

  • Distance and route

    Longer trips cost more in total. Major highway corridors cost less per mile than remote areas.

  • Vehicle size

    Larger and heavier vehicles take more room on the trailer.

  • Trailer type

    Enclosed transport is priced above open.

  • Running condition

    Vehicles that don't run need a winch and extra loading time.

  • Timing and season

    Flexible dates cost less. Rush pickups and peak seasons cost more.

FAQ

Common questions

Have one we didn't cover? Start a quote and ask us about your shipment.

Is my vehicle insured during transport?

Yes. Carriers are required to carry cargo insurance for the vehicles on their trailer. The driver records your vehicle's condition on the bill of lading at pickup and again at delivery, and that record is what any claim is based on.

Do I need to be there for pickup and delivery?

Someone does. You or a person you trust needs to hand over the keys, walk around the vehicle with the driver and sign the bill of lading at both ends.

How should I prepare my vehicle?

Wash it so existing marks are easy to document, remove personal items and toll tags, leave about a quarter tank of fuel, and have one set of keys ready for the driver.

Can I leave personal items in the car?

It's best to ship the vehicle empty. Carrier insurance covers the vehicle and not its contents, and extra weight can affect the price.

Can you ship a vehicle that doesn't run?

Yes. Mark the vehicle as not operable in the quote tool so we assign a carrier with a winch. It needs to roll, steer and brake to be loaded.

How far ahead should I book?

A week or two ahead gives the most carrier options and the best price. If you need it sooner, ask for expedited pickup.
