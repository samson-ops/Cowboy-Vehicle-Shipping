Good to Know

Military Car Shipping Questions

Answers to what customers ask about this service.

Can the truck come on base?
That depends on the installation. Many deliveries happen at the gate or a nearby lot. Tell us the base and we will plan for it.
Can someone else hand over my car if I have already left?
Yes. Name a person you trust and give them the keys. They sign the bill of lading for you.
Do you ship to overseas duty stations?
Our service covers moves within the United States.
What if my orders change?
Tell us as soon as you know and we will adjust the dates.
